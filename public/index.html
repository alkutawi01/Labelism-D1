Getting started

Labelism gives every physical unit (a jersey, a book, a manufactured item) its own QR identity, so you can track it from production through to a customer -- and back, if it's ever returned. Four ideas to know first:

  • Product -- what it is in general (e.g. "Jersey Sekolah ABC").
  • Variant -- a specific version of it (e.g. Size M).
  • Production Batch -- one production run of a variant (e.g. 100 units of Size M).
  • Unit -- one physical, individually-labeled copy from that batch.

Typical order of work:

  1. 1. Tempahan & Label -- Borang pengesahan tempahan. Labelism terus menghasilkan label lengkap mengikut kuantiti pesanan (10 order = 10 label).
  2. 2. Cetak & Tampal Label -- Pasukan pengeluaran mencetak label QR dan mengesahkan penampalan pada pakaian.
  3. 3. Packing & QC -- Imbas label semasa pembungkusan. Rekonsiliasi automatik mengesan sebarang unit tertinggal, lebih, atau tersalah saiz.

Taking an order first? Start with Customers & Orders instead -- Product Setup can link to it.
