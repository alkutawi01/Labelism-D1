Design system

Labelism design system

Every screen in Labelism is built from what is on this page. If you need something that is not here, add it here first, then use it. The full rules are in docs/design-system.md.

Colour Type Space & shape Buttons Forms Feedback Data Workflow Voice

Colour

Tokens only. Light and dark themes switch automatically with the device setting.

Tone means one thing. ok = done or safe, warn = check this, bad = failed or blocking, info = for your information. Brand blue is for actions and selection, never for status.

Type

One family (system UI). Sentence case everywhere: "Print & Attach", not "PRINT & ATTACH".

--fs-2xl 30pxBig number 128
--fs-xl 24pxPage title
--fs-lg 18pxDialog or card title
--fs-md 15pxSection title
--fs-base 14pxBody text and controls. The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.
--fs-sm 13pxSecondary text, hints, dense tables.
--fs-xs 11.5pxPills, table headers. Never for anything a person must read to act.

Space and shape

A 4px grid. Radii: 6px controls, 10px cards, 14px dialogs, pill for status.

Buttons

One primary action per area. Everything else is secondary. Destructive actions are red and always confirm.

Link as button

Forms

Every control has a visible label. Hints explain, errors say what to do.

A hint sits under the field.
Enter a whole number.

Feedback

Inline messages for what is on the page, a toast for a passing confirmation, a dialog only when a decision is needed. Never the browser's own alert or confirm.

Voice and words

English only. Short, plain, in the order people do things.
